# LinkedIn: fractional-cto-for-startups

## Post 1: native text

Your engineers ship every week. They also can't settle an argument about a rewrite, and a customer won't sign until someone answers their security questions.

That team can deliver. What it's missing is someone who owns the technical decisions, so the decisions wait while the work carries on.

A fractional CTO takes that on part time, often a day or two a week: rewrite or refactor, build or buy, which engineers to hire first. Each decision gets written down with its tradeoffs instead of sliding to the next sprint. Engineers get someone senior to go to, and the board update comes from someone who can field the follow-ups.

The title fixes less than you'd hope, though. Whoever you bring in, full time or fractional, agree first which calls they can make, who controls the budget and how disagreements get settled. Without that, a senior person can review your tech, team and roadmap and still leave the same decisions open.

Look at the team you already have, too. A senior engineer may need clearer authority or protected time more than a new boss.

This week, list the last few technical decisions that stalled, who could have made each call and what stopped them. Shape the role from that before you pick a title. The full write-up's in the comments.

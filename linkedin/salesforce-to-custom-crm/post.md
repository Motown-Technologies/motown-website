# LinkedIn: salesforce-to-custom-crm

## Post 1: native text

Your team keeps a spreadsheet next to Salesforce because the CRM no longer fits the work.

Before paying for another round of customization, compare the cost of fixing Salesforce with building and maintaining a custom CRM.

Often the custom CRM loses. If your team is small and your sales process is standard, an off-the-shelf CRM is cheaper than anything custom. If the bill is your only complaint, renegotiate or try a lighter CRM first.

It's worth pricing a custom build when:

• You pay full seats for people who only look things up
• Every change goes through an admin or a consultant
• Automation is spread across workflow rules, Process Builder, Flows and Apex
• Nobody trusts the reports

Compare three years of each. On the Salesforce side, count licenses, add-ons, admin time and consultants. On the custom side, count building and migrating, plus hosting, security updates, backups, support, training and future changes. A custom CRM still needs someone to look after it.

Check your renewal date and notice deadline first, so you know which decisions are due when.

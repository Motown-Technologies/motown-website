# LinkedIn: shopify-custom-or-headless

## Post 1: native text

You pay to bring people to your store, then watch them leave before checkout. The usual pitch is a new platform, and it might not touch the reason they left.

A slow product page, search that fails on a typo and a long checkout each need a different repair. Most of those repairs can happen on the Shopify store you already have.

A custom theme can replace a premium one carrying code from four other themes. Shopify's own checkout tools handle discount, shipping and payment rules where Shopify allows, and custom apps cover what no plugin does.

Before adding another app, look at the ones you have. Remove one at a time and test a purchase after each. Every app was put there for something.

Headless keeps Shopify for carts, orders and tax and swaps out the storefront. It's worth it when the theme can't show what the brand needs, you run a separate store per market, or marketing waits on a developer for every landing page.

The tradeoff is a second system someone has to look after. With a two-person team or a six-week deadline, that's usually more than the store needs.

This week, buy something from your own store on a mid-range phone. Note every point where it gets hard, then ask which of those your current store can fix.
